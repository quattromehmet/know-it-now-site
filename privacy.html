KNOW IT NOW

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy covers Know It Now Uploader (the "App"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Know It Now YouTube channel.

What the App accesses

The App signs in with Google OAuth to the channel owner's own YouTube account only. It requests these permissions:

  • youtube.upload — to upload the channel's own videos.
  • youtube.readonly — to read the channel's own video list and public statistics.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read the channel's own analytics (views, watch time) for a weekly report.

What the App does not do

The App does not access, collect, store, sell or share data of any other Google or YouTube user. It has no public sign-up and is used by one person, the channel owner.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are kept as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and are used only by automated jobs that upload videos and build reports. Weekly channel statistics are stored in that same private repository. Nothing is shared with third parties.

Google API Services

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
